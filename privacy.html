Comail Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 7, 2026

Comail is a personal Google OAuth app. This page explains what data is accessed and how it is used.

Information Accessed

Comail may access Google account data that you explicitly authorize via OAuth scopes, such as Gmail metadata or message content needed for app features.

How Information Is Used

Data is used only to provide and operate Comail features requested by the user.

Data Sharing

Data is not sold. Data is not shared with third parties except when required by law or necessary to operate essential service components.

Data Retention and Deletion

Data is retained only as long as needed to provide app functionality. You may request deletion of your data at any time by email.

Contact
